A Picture of the Work

STORYBOARD SHEET / The Hangar Ops Board, told backwards / draft v1 of the script

A 1940s training film, vertical 1080x1920, about 7 minutes. Narrator: Hoist, one of the little forklifts from the game. The board is always live and moving, never a still, so each frame here is one moment of a moving shot.

BIG BEAT a moment the film holds longerapproximated frame closest saved look, not the exact versionFrames are real renders of the board where one exists

SCENE 2

The objective

0:12 to 0:40

One line of nothing

0:12 to 0:40BIG BEAT

ShotA plain terminal line sits there. Hoist drives up and taps it with a fork.

Hoist“Here's the problem. You ask a crew of AI agents to build your game, and this is all you get. Running. For hours. Running what? Reading what? Stuck? Is it burning your whole week's budget on one file? You can't manage what you can't see.”

On screenRunning agent... 2h 23m

Frame: Drawn terminal frame

Then, one line at a time on screen: "What is each agent doing? / Which tools is it calling? / Is it working or waiting? / What is it costing me?" Hoist: "So we built a picture of the work. Everything on it answers one of those four questions. Let me show you the finished thing first. Then I'll show you how we got here, because it did not start out looking like this."
