# QC notes, cut v4 (long, 3:05)

Reviewer: director / QC pass, fresh eyes. Method: 2 fps frames (370) in _qc/long, 19 timestamped contact sheets in _qc/sheets_long, full-size and 12 fps checks on every doubtful spot (_qc/zoom), OCR of every second frame for "Claude", "cloud" and em dashes, speech-to-text of every voice line with whisper base.en (sherpa-onnx, model fetched from GitHub since Hugging Face is blocked), pause timing from silencedetect on the voice stem, per-second voice vs music level on the mix, EBU loudness, MIDI harmony check of the six cues, frame-difference motion check of the history clips, and a mouth-vs-voice-envelope correlation for lip sync.

## Long vs short: which better meets the owner's intent
The LONG cut is the one to keep working on: it is the only version that answers "what is my crew actually doing?" with the tower carts, the briefing arc, the radio, the Yikes/declutter turn and the cost beat, and at 3:05 it sits inside the 2:55 to 3:10 window the owner asked for.
The SHORT cut (1:41) is tighter and cleaner (no estimate panel, no idle gaps except one) but it drops the tool-call unlock explanation to one line, has a 4.6 s silent hold at 1:13 and a time-lapse that reads as 700x, so it feels like a trailer, not the explainer.
Both share the same five structural faults listed below (pointer at nothing, empty "hectic" board, unreadable unlock, near-silent music in section 4, SFX over words); fix them once in the shared recordings and both cuts improve.

## Verdict: NEEDS ANOTHER PASS
Big step up from v3: Hoist is alive (hops out, points with a stick, lip flap is in sync at zero lag, blinks land in the pauses), the tour framing on one plane works, the time-lapse is the best 20 s in the film, the voice is clean (no heteronym errors, pauses are 0.5 s after sentences and 0.9 s before new ideas, exactly as asked), there is no "Claude" anywhere, no blank frames, and the credit is large. What fails is the owner's number one rule, said = shown, in five places, plus the music in section 4 is effectively inaudible and the sound effects sit on top of words.

## Fixes, most important first

1. 0:36.0 to 0:38.0, scene 3 (camera truck). Hoist says "And that camera truck is filming. See the number?" while the stick is retracted into his box pointing at nothing; the camera truck has driven out of frame and only re-enters at 0:38. Same at 0:41.0 to 0:43.0 ("Beacon on, it's busy": stick in the box for 2 s) and 0:32.5 to 0:35.5 (baggage cart and tug are at the far bottom-left edge under the legend, the stick swings to the frame corner). Root cause: the tour was recorded on a live board where vehicles cross the whole frame in about 2 s (frame diff 9.6 per half second, same order as the 150x replay), so every target wanders. Fix: record the tour with the board paused or at 0.25x (or script the vehicles to park beside the plane for the shot), so each named vehicle sits still inside the frame while it is named, and the stick lands once and holds. If a target is ever off-screen, do not retract the stick: cut the line or move the camera.

2. 1:54.2 to 2:00.0, scene 4 (V22 "Then carts. Lots of carts. It looked hectic. Yikes!"). The board shows an almost empty field: the tower, three cards and about six tiny carts (see _qc/long/f0236.png). Nothing looks hectic, so the turning point of the story and the one slapstick yelp land on nothing. Fix: pick a V22 moment with dozens of carts on screen (the V22 recording or the live-events log should have a busy minute), frame it at 1.3x on the hub so the carts read, and let the clutter build over the 6 s so "Yikes!" at 1:58.9 hits the peak. If no busy V22 moment exists, spawn carts in the V22 recreation; the point is visual density.

3. 2:00.0 to 2:11.2, scene 4 (V23, the unlock). "An icon for each kind of work. A light for working or waiting. And for the first time you could say: that one's reading, that one's filming." Icons and beacons are 4 to 5 px on a dark wide board and nothing is pointed at; Hoist stays in his box. This is the beat the whole history builds to and it fails the mute test. Fix: push in to about 1.5x on the cluster beside the orchestrator (this is the one place a zoom is earned), have Hoist hop out with the stick and tap a reading cart on "that one's reading" (2:08.5) and a filming cart on "that one's filming" (2:09.8), each with a ripple, and hold the frame still while he does it.

4. 1:28 to 1:46, scene 4 music. Cue 4 starts with celesta only and the cue is normalised to its loud brass ending, so for the first 18 s the music sits at minus 45 to minus 52 dB under the voice and minus 43 dB in the gaps, against minus 28 to minus 35 dB in every other section. On a phone this is silence; the "assembly cue that grows" is heard as the music dropping out, and it only becomes audible with the boing at 1:58.9. Fix: normalise each cue by RMS (or loudness), not peak, and start cue 4 at no more than 8 dB below its ending (celesta plus pizz from bar 1), so the growth is audible rather than from nothing.

5. 0:57.9, 1:12.5, 1:14.8, 1:27.9, 2:41.0, 2:46.5: sound effects as loud as the voice and on top of words. The whoosh at 0:57.9 (minus 19.8 dB, the voice averages minus 20) runs under "Up top", the two whooshes at 1:12.5 and 1:14.8 sit under "When it hands out a job" and "fly down the taxi line", the slide whistle at 1:27.9 under "It started simple", the slide at 2:41.0 and whoosh at 2:46.5 at minus 20. 14. Lip flap shape holds. Sync is good (mouth vs voice envelope correlation 0.78 to 0.83, zero lag) but the mouth is driven by loudness, so one open shape is held for up to 10 frames (0.8 s) in steady vowels, which reads as a frozen mouth. Fix: drive the mouth from syllable onsets (envelope peaks at 4 to 7 Hz), close between syllables, and cap any held shape at 4 frames.

15. Music harmony and section joins. The algorithmic cues have melodies that do not follow the chords: semitone clashes between sustained notes in 25% of eighth-note frames in cue 5, 16% in cue 1, 15% in cue 3 (cue 2: 1%, cue 6: 8%). Section joins crossfade 1.2 s across unrelated keys: D major into E major at 2:24.7 and E major into F major at 2:46.7, so the final brass chord of one cue rings against the next cue's first chord a semitone away. Fix: constrain melody notes on beats to chord tones (passing tones only on off-beats), write cue 6 in A or E so the close follows cue 5, and replace the 1.2 s crossfade with a 0.15 s cut on the downbeat (or end each cue on the next cue's dominant). Nobody has heard this by ear yet; MJ should listen to cues 1, 3 and 5 in isolation before the next render.

16. Loudness. Integrated loudness is minus 20.5 LUFS with true peak about minus 0.6 dB. Phone and social players expect about minus 16 (YouTube only turns down, never up), so the film plays quiet. Fix: loudnorm to minus 16 LUFS, true peak minus 1.5 dB, after the SFX and music fixes above.
