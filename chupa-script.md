## Visual notes that differ from the Hoist cut
- Lower-left host box shows El Chupacabra's parked, face-framed talking clip (mouth driven by his voice envelope). No forklift, no pointer. Spot rings stay.
- Scene 2: Rochelle's full-model card says hello to him, he asks why she looks flat, the low-poly card swaps in and she gives the polygon-diet line; he answers.
- Scene 4: no plane-size chip, no size tags, no plane-size narration. Deadpan: his face holds still, eyes to camera, for "Started with planes, ... ended with planes."
- Scene 6 (MJ notes 11 to 13): the plane on the Making this video stand is Baby Beluga (biggest job, biggest plane, small jobs get small planes). He goes down to the runway; the view shifts to a ground-level 3D shot of the two parked at an angle, both faces talking, crew vehicles working behind them. Banter: her size joke ("big me", "your own weather"), her quip, the cargo-door ride offer (her nose swings open) and his dramatic refusal. The crew interview (script cart, camera truck, QC tug) is folded in as quick cutaways to the board. Sign-off, then the silent credit card.
